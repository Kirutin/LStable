# Warehouse-13 rEFInd palette

- Neon pink: `#ff0f54`
- Magenta: `#f018a9`
- Violet: `#7c55ff`
- Background base: black with a subtle violet cast

The Debian icon, selection indicators, navigation arrows and firmware/tool
icons use the pink-to-violet gradient. Other operating-system icons remain
neutral white so that additional loaders stay legible and visually distinct.
